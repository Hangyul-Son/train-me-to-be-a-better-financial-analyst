Finance

Fundamentals

Financial Statement Analysis

Understanding the three core financial statements — income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement — and how they connect to tell the story of a company's financial health.

  • Income Statement: Revenue, expenses, and profitability over a period
  • Balance Sheet: Assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time
  • Cash Flow Statement: How cash moves in and out of the business
Valuation

Key Financial Ratios

Ratios that help compare companies and assess performance regardless of size.

  • P/E Ratio: Price-to-Earnings — how much investors pay per dollar of earnings
  • ROE: Return on Equity — how effectively a company uses shareholder capital
  • Debt-to-Equity: Leverage indicator — how much debt vs. equity funds the company
  • Current Ratio: Liquidity measure — can the company cover short-term obligations?
Markets

How Markets Work

Core concepts about how financial markets operate and how prices are determined.

  • Supply & Demand: The fundamental driver of all market prices
  • Market Efficiency: The EMH debate — are all known facts priced in?
  • Risk & Return: Higher expected returns require taking on more risk

Tech

Programming

Languages & Tools

Key programming languages and tools relevant to finance and data analysis.

  • Python: The go-to for data analysis, modeling, and automation
  • SQL: Essential for querying and managing financial databases
  • Excel / Google Sheets: Still the backbone of financial modeling
  • Git: Version control for tracking changes in code and models
Data

Data Analysis for Finance

Techniques for extracting insights from financial data.

  • Pandas: Data manipulation and cleaning in Python
  • Visualization: Matplotlib, Seaborn for charts and trend analysis
  • APIs: Pulling real-time market data programmatically
  • Regression: Statistical modeling to find relationships in data
AI / ML

AI in Finance

How artificial intelligence and machine learning are transforming financial analysis.

  • Sentiment Analysis: NLP on earnings calls and news to gauge market mood
  • Algorithmic Trading: Automated strategies based on quantitative signals
  • Risk Modeling: ML models for credit scoring and fraud detection
